# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in Parapet to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds a good environment:

- Being kind and patient with people who are learning
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Using the project, its lab, or its tooling to attack systems without
  authorisation
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate
reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported privately to the maintainers listed in
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS). All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the
privacy and security of the reporter.

## Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private warning, with an explanation of the impact.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including
   a period without interaction.
3. **Temporary ban** — a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban** — a permanent ban from any public interaction with the
   project.
