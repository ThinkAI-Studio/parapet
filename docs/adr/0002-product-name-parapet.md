# 0002. Product name: Parapet

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-26
- **Deciders:** TV1, TV2, TV3

## Context

The repository belongs to ThinkAI Studio and is public from day one. The name
has to work as a GitHub repository, a domain, a badge label, and a word a
Vietnamese speaker can pronounce on the first try.

It must also describe what the product does: look at your defences from the
outside, from a fixed, slightly elevated position.

## Decision

The product is called **Parapet**. The repository is
`ThinkAI-Studio/parapet`, the primary domain is `parapet.dev`, and the
vulnerable-by-design lab is **Parapet Range** at `lab.parapet.dev`.

A parapet is the low wall along the edge of a roof or rampart: you can look over
it, but you cannot fall. It is the outermost defensive layer — which is exactly
the surface the product assesses.

## Consequences

**Positive**

- Short, two syllables, unambiguous pronunciation.
- Not claimed by a major security vendor.
- Supports a coherent family: Parapet Range, Parapet Bench, Parapet Badge.
- Sits naturally under the studio name: *Parapet by ThinkAI Studio*.

**Negative**

- Slightly abstract for non-native English speakers; the tagline carries the
  meaning.
- No strong Vietnamese association.

**Neutral**

- The tagline "Prove your attack surface" does the explanatory work.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Ravelin** — semantically the best fit (an outwork *in front of* the main
   fortification), but harder to remember and spell.
2. **Rampart** — strong, but heavily used in games and fantasy.
3. **Posture** — clearest in the security domain, but collides with the generic
   term "security posture" and is hard to search for.
4. **Bastion** — already strongly associated with Azure Bastion.
5. **Outpost** — good meaning, bland and common.

## References

- Merriam-Webster: *parapet* — a wall of earth or stone to protect soldiers; a
  low wall at the edge of a roof, balcony or bridge.
